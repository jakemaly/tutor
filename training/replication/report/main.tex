\section{Overview}
We replicate the multi-turn RL tutoring method of Dinucu-Jianu et al.\ (EMNLP 2025) with the Qwen3.5-9B tutor
(bf16 LoRA) in place of Qwen2.5-7B. The data split, prompts, reward ($r_{\text{sol}} + (r_{\text{ped}}-1)\lambda + r_{\text{templ}}$, $\lambda=0.75$),
batch shape (16 problems $\times$ 8 rollouts, $\mu=2$, $\beta=0.001$, 300 policy updates), student, and judges follow the paper.
Training and evaluation run on molab; GPU targets are pending a run.
